PageNarrator modifications: selected complete recordings are converted to mono
24 kHz, zero-padded to a 1920-sample boundary where required, and encoded as
OmniVoice codec tokens. Source metadata in src/tts/omnivoice/speaker-data.js
identifies the dataset revision, split, speaker ID, source file, audio hash,
encoder hash and preprocessing for each reference.

The reference data keep their source license, separately from the PageNarrator
implementation and the OmniVoice model.
