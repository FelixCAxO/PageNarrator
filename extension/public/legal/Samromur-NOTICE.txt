PageNarrator bundles OmniVoice-generated references derived from Samromur
parent references. The cards retain generated codec tokens and synthesis text,
not complete source recordings. Source metadata in
src/tts/omnivoice/speaker-data.js retain the dataset revision, reader ID,
source filename, attribution and URL, source-audio and encoder hashes, parent
reference IDs/hash, and generation settings/output-audio hash. Split and
parent preprocessing records are not retained in the current catalog; it is
not a complete source archive.

The reference data keep their source license, separately from the PageNarrator
implementation and the OmniVoice model.
