Underlying recordings are from LibriVox. LibriTTS-R's publisher applied
Miipher speech restoration before distributing the recordings.

PageNarrator bundles OmniVoice-generated references derived from LibriTTS-R
parent references. The cards retain generated codec tokens and synthesis
text, not complete source utterances. Source metadata in
src/tts/omnivoice/speaker-data.js retain the reader ID, dataset attribution
and URL, source-audio and encoder hashes, parent reference IDs/hash, and
generation settings/output-audio hash. Seven of the 23 current cards also
retain an original recording filename and dataset revision; the other 16
do not. Archive filenames and parent preprocessing records are not retained.
A parent hash alone does not locate an original corpus utterance; this
catalog is not a complete source archive.

The recordings and derived reference data retain CC BY 4.0. This does not change
the separate licenses of PageNarrator's code or the OmniVoice model.
