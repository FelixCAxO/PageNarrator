PageNarrator retains complete publisher utterances and their published
transcripts. The recordings are resampled from mono 48 kHz to mono 24 kHz,
padded with zeros where needed for codec boundaries, and encoded as voice
references. The adapted reference data retain CC BY-SA 4.0. These changes
do not imply endorsement by the original contributors.

Per-reference metadata retain the published reader and archive member IDs,
member CRC, input audio hash, archive index hash, transcript file and hash,
publisher page hash, encoder hash and preprocessing description. The index
hash identifies the archived directory evidence; it is not a claim that the
whole publisher ZIP was downloaded or hashed. If a generated recording is
used as a derived reference, its parent reference and generation parameters
must also be retained.

The reference-data license is separate from the model and application-code
licenses. A release containing these references must include this notice
and the full CC BY-SA 4.0 license text.
