PageNarrator modifications: selected complete annotated utterances are extracted
from the published participant channel, converted to mono 24 kHz, padded to a
1920-sample boundary where needed, and encoded as OmniVoice codec tokens.
Where indicated in the per-reference metadata, quiet utterances are raised to
RMS 0.1 before encoding. The original recording hash, measured input RMS,
applied gain, preparation-code hash and encoder-input hash are retained.
Source metadata retains the published reader ID, recording and annotation
identifiers, source boundaries, source and encoder hashes, and preprocessing.
Generated references derived from these recordings retain the source attribution,
parent reference hash and generation settings.
