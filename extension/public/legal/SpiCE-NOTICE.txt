PageNarrator bundles OmniVoice-generated references derived from SpiCE parent
references. The cards retain generated codec tokens and synthesis text, not
complete source utterances. Source metadata in src/tts/omnivoice/speaker-data.js
retain the dataset revision, reader ID, original-file identifier, attribution
and URL, source-audio and encoder hashes, parent reference IDs/hash, and
generation settings/output-audio hash. Annotation identifiers, source-channel
boundaries, gain/RMS measurements and parent preprocessing records are not
retained in the current catalog; it is not a complete source archive.
