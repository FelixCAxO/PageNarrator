PageNarrator modifications: selected complete recordings are converted to mono
24 kHz, zero-padded to a 1920-sample boundary where required, and encoded as
OmniVoice codec tokens. Source metadata identifies the dataset revision, shard,
row, speaker ID, source filename, audio hash, encoder hash and preprocessing.

All recorded references from all sources were cleaned with NVIDIA NemotronLabs
RE-USE speech enhancement (https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-NemotronLabs-RE-USE,
OpenMDW-1.1) and trimmed of leading and trailing silence before encoding; each
reference records the model revision, the input and output audio hashes, and the trim. The enhancement adds no license terms to the
reference data, which keep their source license.
