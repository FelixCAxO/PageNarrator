PageNarrator bundles OmniVoice-generated references derived from NST Swedish
parent references. The cards retain generated codec tokens and synthesis
text, not complete source recordings. Source metadata in
src/tts/omnivoice/speaker-data.js retain the dataset revision/configuration,
source filename, reader ID, attribution and URL, source-audio and encoder
hashes, parent reference IDs/hash, and generation settings/output-audio hash.
Shard/row locations and parent preprocessing records are not retained in the
current catalog; it is not a complete source archive.
