A pace trial remains separate from catalog qualification until its exact setting is supported and verified throughout playback.

If a section exceeds the 2,048-position inference limit or the decoder's GPU buffer limit, the playback adapter splits it at sentence, word or grapheme boundaries and synthesizes the parts in order with the same preset and sampling seed. Each part passes the normal signal check before concatenation. Cancellation stops the remaining parts. This preserves measured pacing for slower references and handles number-heavy sections without reloading the model or substituting a voice. Reference generation still requires a single directly decoded codec sequence.

## Development screening

Reference selection starts with the highest-scoring passing candidate, then prefers the largest minimum cosine distance from the references already selected. Each choice stays within 0.25 DNSMOS overall points of the best remaining eligible candidate. Transcript failures remain behind fully passing references, and the existing signal, acoustic and gender checks still apply. This uses the measured speaker embeddings to spread the numbered variations; different codec hashes alone do not establish different speaker identities. Neither metric qualifies perceived naturalness. The final joint selector maximizes minimum speaker distance, then mean acoustic quality and mean distance among the measured passing options. It retains original acoustic and distance floors, a 0.25 cohort quality band, unique known readers and code hashes, and cross-text nearest-identity matching across all twenty voices in each language. Search counts and limits are retained in the selection report.
