How it works

Choose text. Press play.

PageNarrator extracts text and reads it aloud on your device.

1. Choose what to read

Use Read page for the current visible section or saved position. Use Read selected for a highlighted passage. PageNarrator removes navigation and page clutter where it can.

2. Prepare the voice

The built-in Kokoro voices run on your device. Missing files download from Hugging Face. WebGPU handles speech when available; CPU / WebAssembly is the slower fallback. Experimental OmniVoice needs WebGPU with fp16 support and its own download.

Cached files work offline for the same voice and mode. Clearing storage or changing mode may require another download.

3. Follow along

Highlights follow the speech. Use the transcript, pause, jump between sections, or change speed. Playback continues when you close the popup. Your reading position is saved locally.

4. Read PDFs

The PDF viewer keeps the document visible while you listen. Select text, change pages, zoom, or rotate. English scans use local OCR.
