PageNarrator 1.0.1

Read aloud. Keep your place.

Read webpages, selected text, and PDFs aloud. Speech runs on your device. Free. No account or usage quota. Version 1.0.1 source is available on GitHub; Chrome Web Store availability is pending.

Recommended: a dedicated NVIDIA RTX 30 series GPU or newer, with 8 GB+ VRAM and 8 GB+ system RAM.

Made for long reads

Reading tools

Read the useful part

Extract the main text from articles and guides. Leave page clutter out where possible.

Open a PDF and listen

Keep the PDF visible while you listen. Select text, zoom, or rotate.

Follow along

Highlights follow the speech on the page and in the transcript.

Listen your way

Set speed from 0.5× to 3×. Skip code, adjust pauses, and choose highlight colors.

Come back later

Resume where you stopped. Use the transcript to find a section.

Local by default

Built-in voices run on your device

Missing voice files download from Hugging Face. Cached files work offline for the same voice and mode; clearing storage or changing mode may require another download. PageNarrator does not receive your reading text, PDFs, URLs, or browsing history for narration.
