Privacy

What stays local

Speech runs on your device. PageNarrator does not receive your reading text for narration.

Reading and storage

Webpage text, selected text, and PDFs are processed in your browser. Settings and voice choices stay in extension-local storage. Resume saves the URL, title, position, and a short text excerpt locally.

Voice downloads

Missing voice files download from Hugging Face. Its delivery hosts receive your IP address and requested file paths, not narration text. Cached files work offline for the same voice and mode. Changing either, clearing storage, or browser eviction can require another download.

Online documents

Your browser fetches online pages and PDFs from their original hosts. Those requests follow the hosts' privacy terms. Fetching a document is separate from generating speech.

Website and support

No account is needed. The website has no advertising or analytics scripts. Hosting and font providers receive ordinary web requests. If you contact support, we receive your email address and message.
