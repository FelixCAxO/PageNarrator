Follow the text

Highlights follow the speech. Use the transcript to find a section, adjust speed, or resume where you stopped.

Skip code when needed

Turn on Skip code snippets for code-heavy pages. Adjust section pauses and highlight colors in Advanced settings.

Keep internal docs local

Speech runs on your device. PageNarrator does not receive the text, URL, or browsing history for narration.
