Research

Listen to research papers

Recommended: a dedicated NVIDIA RTX 30 series GPU or newer, with 8 GB+ VRAM and 8 GB+ system RAM.

Read papers and reports aloud while keeping figures, citations, and the page in view.

Follow the paper

Highlights follow the speech. Use the transcript to revisit a section, adjust speed up to 3×, and resume where you stopped.

Check recognized text

Scanned pages use local English OCR. Recognition accuracy depends on scan quality and layout. Check the text against the page, especially formulas, tables, and columns.

Keep unpublished work local

Speech and PDF text extraction run on your device. PageNarrator does not receive the document, URL, or reading text for narration. Online PDFs are fetched from their host.
