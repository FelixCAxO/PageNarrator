PDFs

Listen to a PDF

Recommended: a dedicated NVIDIA RTX 30 series GPU or newer, with 8 GB+ VRAM and 8 GB+ system RAM.

Keep the document visible while PageNarrator reads it aloud.

Open and read

Open a PDF and start reading from the toolbar. Local files need Allow access to file URLs in the extension's details.

Use highlights and the transcript to follow the text. Change speed, jump between sections, zoom, rotate, or resume later.

Scanned pages

Scanned PDFs use local English OCR. Recognition accuracy depends on scan quality and layout. Check the recognized text against the page. Unsupported formats and resource limits return an error.

Privacy

Text extraction and built-in narration happen in your browser. PageNarrator does not receive your PDF or reading text for narration. Online PDFs are fetched from their document host.
