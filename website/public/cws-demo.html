Research brief

Reading long documents

Local speech with highlights that follow the text.

Overview

Read a report while keeping the page and your notes in view. Playback, transcript, and queue controls stay in the toolbar.

PageNarrator extracts the article text and leaves repeated navigation out. Highlights follow each spoken passage.

Key observation: use the highlights to follow the text, check a heading, or return to a passage.

Reader workflow

  • Open PageNarrator from the toolbar.
  • Start reading. Use the transcript to choose a section.
  • Choose highlight colors in Advanced settings.
  • Close and reopen the popup while speech continues.

PDFs use the same playback controls. Follow the passage, change speed, and resume where you stopped.

Privacy note

Speech runs on your device. PageNarrator does not receive your reading text, PDFs, URLs, or browsing history for narration.
