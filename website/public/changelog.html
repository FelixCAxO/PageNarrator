Release notes

Changelog

Features and known limits by version.

Version 1.0.2

Download v1.0.2Get the sourceRelease notes
  • Fixed voices in 32 languages, stable across documents and restarts.
  • Improved PDF row order, wrapped cells, and Swedish characters.
  • Experimental whisper uses the selected voice. Cantonese and Thai can omit words.
  • Speech arithmetic fixes. Listening quality and hardware performance remain unverified.

Version 1.0.1

Reliability maintenance version. Build from source.

  • Voice download progress, cancellable setup, and clean retry.
  • Bounded PDF fallback downloads with cancellation.
  • Recovery from stalled Kokoro workers.
  • Incognito access disabled until reading metadata has separate private handling.
  • Clearer ZIP updates and current product screenshots.

Existing numerical, hardware, and human-review limitations remain disclosed; their exceptions were explicitly extended to this release. They do not apply to v1.0.2.

Version 1.0.0

Earlier release downloads are no longer available. Chrome Web Store availability is pending.

  • Webpages, selected text, and PDF reading.
  • Ten local English voices and experimental multilingual OmniVoice.
  • Highlights, transcript, saved position, and 0.5× to 3× speed.
  • Local English OCR for scanned PDFs.
  • Voice settings, reading cleanup, and section pauses.

Downloads and privacy

Missing voice files download from Hugging Face. Speech runs on your device. Cached files work offline for the same voice and mode; clearing the cache may require another download. No account is required.

Privacy details.

Known limits

Scan accuracy depends on the page. Experimental language quality varies. Physical hardware tiers and broad listening quality are unverified. Kokoro still differs from the reference in duration and audio samples.
