Privacy policy

Privacy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Reading data

PageNarrator processes text, PDFs, and highlights in your browser. Built-in Kokoro voices run locally. Experimental OmniVoice also runs on your device. English OCR files ship in the extension. PageNarrator does not receive reading content, URLs, or browsing history for narration.

Built-in voice downloads

Missing files download from Hugging Face and its delivery hosts. They receive your IP address and requested files. Hugging Face does not receive narration text. Files are stored in the extension's browser cache and work offline for the same voice and mode, including after a restart. Changing voice or mode, clearing storage, or browser eviction may require another download. Executable code ships in the extension.

Online documents

Online PDFs are requested from their document host. That host receives the URL and normal connection information, and may receive existing browser credentials needed to open the document. This is separate from speech generation.

Extension storage

Local extension storage holds preferences, voice choices, diagnostics, page and PDF URLs and titles, positions, and a text excerpt of up to 160 characters per saved position. Model files use the browser cache. These are not synced to PageNarrator servers.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Reading content, URLs, settings, voices, diagnostics, and positions are used only for narration, highlighting, and resume. PageNarrator does not sell this data, use it for advertising or lending, transfer it for unrelated purposes, or allow humans to read it. PageNarrator follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Website and support

The website has no advertising or behavioral tracking scripts. Cloudflare processes hosting logs. Pages using Google Fonts send font requests and normal connection information to Google. Support emails are used to reply and keep support records.

Remove your data

Upgrades remove retired custom-voice settings, API keys, and imported models. Interrupted cleanup retries automatically; data remains until it finishes. Remove local data and voice files by clearing the extension's storage or uninstalling it.

For access, correction, deletion of support correspondence, or other privacy questions, email support@pagenarrator.com.
