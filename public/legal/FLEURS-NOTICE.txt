PageNarrator modifications: selected complete recordings are converted to mono
24 kHz, zero-padded to a 1920-sample boundary where required, and encoded as
OmniVoice codec tokens. Any generated reference derived from a recording retains
its source and parent reference hash. Source metadata identifies the dataset
revision, language configuration, split, source filename, audio/metadata/archive
hashes, encoder hash and preprocessing. Published reference transcripts are also
used as independent development test text, with source metadata retained.
