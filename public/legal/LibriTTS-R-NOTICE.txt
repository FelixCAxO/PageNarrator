Nobuyuki Morioka, Michiel Bacchiani, Yu Zhang, Wei Han, and Ankur Bapna,
"LibriTTS-R: A Restored Multi-Speaker Text-to-Speech Corpus," 2023.
Underlying recordings are from LibriVox. LibriTTS-R's publisher applied
Miipher speech restoration before distributing the recordings.

PageNarrator modifications: selected complete utterances are converted to mono
24 kHz audio, zero-padded to a 1920-sample boundary when needed, and encoded
as OmniVoice codec tokens. References generated from a recording retain its
attribution and the parent reference hash. Per-reference source metadata in
src/tts/omnivoice/speaker-data.js identifies the reader, recording, source audio,
archive, encoder, processing, and any subsequent generation.

The recordings and derived reference data retain CC BY 4.0. This does not change
the separate licenses of PageNarrator's code or the OmniVoice model.
